Approved viewers

Signed-in organization teammates get access automatically on first open - no approval needed. Removing someone blocks them from auto access until you add them back here. Use the viewer dropdown (or click a viewer) to filter the activity table below. "Live" means their dashboard tab was open in the last ~2 minutes: open tabs ping every minute while visible, so a closed, hidden or offline tab stops showing as Live on its own. It shows the tab is open, not that the person is actively reading.
